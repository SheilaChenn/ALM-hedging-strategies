# Exclusions and intended use

This repository is a research and demonstration framework. It is not a Pacific
Life model, a production actuarial system, an accounting valuation, a trade
recommendation, or investment advice.

The following are deliberately outside scope:

- actual policyholder, carrier, dealer, or market data;
- guaranteed living/withdrawal benefits and other complex riders;
- dynamic lapse, withdrawal, premium, allocation, and anti-selection behavior;
- policy loans, taxes, surrender charges, and detailed death-benefit corridors;
- stochastic mortality, morbidity, and longevity;
- stochastic rates, volatility, dividends, basis, correlation, and regime shifts;
- counterparty default, collateral, funding, capital, liquidity, and market impact;
- legal contract interpretation and state/product-specific provisions;
- statutory, GAAP, LDTI, IFRS 17, tax, and economic-capital measurement;
- operational trade execution, approvals, limit monitoring, and reconciliation;
- empirical hedge effectiveness or performance claims.

Synthetic outputs can look realistic but are not observations. Hedge results are
conditional on simplified models and constraints and can understate model,
basis, behavior, liquidity, and gap risk. Decisions require approved data,
independent model validation, governance, and qualified actuarial, risk, legal,
accounting, and investment review.
